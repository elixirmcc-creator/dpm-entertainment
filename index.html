KNOWLEDGE MODULES

Grand Reveal Mosaic

Essential insights into the most prestigious luxury pageant in the nation. Discover the path to the crown through our curated knowledge modules.

What are the eligibility criteria for participating in DPM INDIA 2026?

stat_minus_1
Participants must be Indian citizens, aged between 18 and 28 years as of January 1, 2026. Unmarried status is required for the Miss category, while specific marital criteria apply to Mrs. categories. A minimum height of 5'5" without heels is preferred, though exceptional talent may warrant a waiver.

How do I submit my application?

stat_minus_1
Applications must be submitted exclusively through our official portal. Ensure you have high-resolution portfolio images, an introductory video, and valid identification documents ready before commencing the process.

What is the selection process?

stat_minus_1
The journey begins with digital portfolio screening, followed by regional virtual auditions. Shortlisted candidates will then be invited to our exclusive grooming workshops in Mumbai before the grand finale.

What does the comprehensive grooming program entail?

stat_minus_1
Our intensive 14-day grooming residency covers advanced runway techniques, editorial posing, public speaking, etiquette, and personal branding. Industry stalwarts, including supermodels, acclaimed designers, and communication experts, personally mentor the finalists.

Is there an entry fee?

stat_minus_1
A nominal processing fee of ₹2,500 is required upon application submission. Should you be selected as a finalist, a comprehensive participation package will be detailed, covering accommodation, grooming, and wardrobe.

Who comprises the judging panel?

stat_minus_1
The jury consists of luminaries from the fashion, entertainment, and business sectors. Past panels have included renowned couturiers, leading editorial directors, and celebrated actors.

Can international residents apply?

stat_minus_1
Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) and Overseas Citizens of India (OCIs) are welcome to apply, provided they hold valid documentation and can attend the mandatory in-person phases in India.

What are the key dates?

stat_minus_1
Applications close on October 31, 2025. Regional auditions will be conducted in November, and the grand finale is scheduled for February 2026 in New Delhi.

What opportunities await the winner?

stat_minus_1
The titleholder receives the prestigious DPM crown, a cash prize, an annual contract with Elite Media Group, and the opportunity to represent India at select international pageants. Furthermore, they gain exclusive access to premier fashion weeks and editorial shoots.

How can I contact support?

stat_minus_1
For technical assistance with your application or specific queries not addressed here, please contact our concierge desk at concierge@dpmindia.com.
